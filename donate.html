Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about donating to the Destiny John Foundation.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes! The Destiny John Foundation is a registered non-profit organization. You'll receive an official tax receipt via email for every donation.

How is my donation used?

100% of your donation goes directly to our programs: pediatric healthcare, education supplies, nutrition support, and community development.

How does monthly giving work?

Choose Monthly, then complete the steps on this page each month. There is no automatic deduction, so you stay in control and can stop whenever you like.

How do I get my receipt?

Once we verify your screenshot, we'll email your receipt to the address you provided. If you don't see it, check your spam folder or contact us.

Is my information secure?

We only ask for the details on the form so we can verify your donation and send your receipt. We never ask for card numbers or PINs, and card payments are processed by Flutterwave.

Can I donate in honor of someone?

Yes! Use the tribute option in the form to dedicate your donation in memory of a loved one, and we'll note the dedication with your donation.
