Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We typically respond to emails within 24 hours during business days. WhatsApp messages receive even faster replies, usually within 2 hours.

Can I schedule a meeting with your team?

Absolutely! Send us an email with your preferred date and time, and we'll arrange a meeting either in person at our Freetown office or via video call.

Do you accept walk-in visitors?

Yes, our office is open to visitors during business hours. We recommend calling ahead so we can ensure the right team member is available to assist you.

How can I report an issue with my donation?

Please email donations@destinyjohnfoundation.org with your receipt number and details. Our team will investigate and resolve the issue within 48 hours.

Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes! We have many remote volunteer opportunities including translation, design, fundraising, and administrative support. Email volunteer@destinyjohnfoundation.org.

Do you offer school or group visits?

We'd love to host your group! Contact us at least 2 weeks in advance to arrange an educational visit to learn about our programs and impact.

Ready to Make a Difference?

Whether you want to donate, volunteer, or partner with us, every connection helps us create lasting change for children in Sierra Leone.
